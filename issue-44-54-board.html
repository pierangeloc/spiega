#44 remarks and #54 step groups

The flow

  1. 1You click a box and type; the page writes a remark op
  2. 2A new `remark` op: {at, text, by}
  3. 3Appended, so a discussed board replays as a discussion
  4. 4Every open viewer is patched
  5. 5Drawn through the existing callout geometry
  6. 6The agent pulls on the next follow-up — MCP cannot be interrupted
  7. 7The rail header: testable in node, an agent can finish it
  8. 8The third tier — the half nobody can self-certify

What was said

  • #44 turns the board page from a reader into a writer. Everything else here already works.
  • step 1The page already POSTs ops — today only focus. A human remark rides the same proven path.
  • step 2Keep remark its own op — it is a reply, not part of the explanation, and the agent must not be able to remove it.
  • step 3Remarks go in the same transcript, with a by field — the interleaving of said and drawn is what makes replay worth having.
  • step 3That walk counts transcript entries positionally. Filter by op kind, or every highlight on the board quietly goes wrong.
  • step 4And here is hazard two: the patch re-renders the SVG. A text input inside the SVG is wiped out mid-sentence by an agent still drawing.
  • step 5A remark *is* a callout in a different hand. New op, no new renderer — the geometry is already there and already tested.
  • step 6MCP is request/response, so nothing can push. Phase 1 is the pull model, and the line in the skill matters more than the code.
  • step 6Return remarks with indices and advance a watermark — never let unseen be something the agent can reset.
  • #54 lands in the same place — render.js is where both features meet.
  • step 7A step names its group; the group does not list its steps. Indices renumber on insert, a key does not.
  • step 8Not three opacity tiers: two axes. Opacity says in-the-phase or not; orange and halo say this-is-the-step.
  • Four decisions before delegating: non-contiguous groups, whether to split #54 into two PRs, #44 phase-1 scope, and the order.
  • My answers: span with repeated headers; split #54; phase 1 only, no regions; #54 first, because it is days and #44 is weeks.
